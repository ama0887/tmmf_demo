What tokenized money market funds are, how they work, and why you might care.

The problem

Your spare cash is probably napping.

The basics

A money market fund pools cash and lends it out for short stretches.

The old plumbing

Behind the scenes, it runs on bankers' hours.

The change

Tokenization records each share as a token on a blockchain.

What changes for you

It moves any hour.

Why care

Cash that earns while it waits, and moves when you need it.

The fine print

New rails, real risks.

  • Not a bank account. Not insured by the FDIC (Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation).
  • Rare, but money market funds have dipped below $1 a share in past crises.
  • Today, access is often limited to approved, verified investors.
  • New tech, new risks: code bugs, hacks, lost keys.
  • Turning tokens back into dollars may still follow the fund's hours.
